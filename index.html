TOOL 01 / FORCE SYSTEM

Resultant of coplanar forces

Resolve forces into x- and y-components, then find the resultant magnitude and direction.

ΣFₓ = Σ(F cos θ) | ΣFᵧ = Σ(F sin θ)
R = √[(ΣFₓ)² + (ΣFᵧ)²]
TOOL 02 / FRICTION

Block on a rough horizontal surface

Explore the relationship between applied force, normal reaction and friction.

N = mg | Fₘₐₓ = μN
Static friction balances applied force up to its limiting value.
TOOL 03 / EQUILIBRIUM

Lami’s theorem explorer

For three concurrent, coplanar forces in equilibrium, each force is proportional to the sine of the angle between the other two forces.

P / sin α = Q / sin β = R / sin γ
Learning model: input angles are normalised to 360° for the illustration. In a real problem, use the actual included angles between the other two forces shown in your diagram.

A repeatable method

How to solve a mechanics problem

Use the same disciplined process for most introductory statics questions.

01

Isolate the body

Draw a clear free-body diagram. Replace contacts and supports with their appropriate reaction forces.

02

Choose axes

Resolve each force along x and y. Write the angle relative to the axis you are using.

03

Apply equilibrium

For static equilibrium, use ΣFₓ = 0, ΣFᵧ = 0 and, when needed, ΣM = 0.

KEY CONCEPTS

Remember these

Force: a vector quantity with magnitude and direction.

Resultant: one force with the same combined effect as a force system.

Equilibrium: the vector sum of forces and sum of moments are zero for a rigid body in static equilibrium.

Friction: acts along the contact surface and opposes relative motion or its tendency.

Moment: turning effect of a force; magnitude is force × perpendicular distance.
